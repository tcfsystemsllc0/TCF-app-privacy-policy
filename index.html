Privacy Policy — Invoice Maker

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What this app does

Invoice Maker lets you create and manage business invoices on your phone: clients, line items, totals, due dates, and paid status.

Data collection

Invoice Maker collects no data. There is no account, no sign-in, no backend server, and no analytics. Everything you enter — business name, client names, invoice items, and amounts — is stored only in a file on your own device and never leaves it.

Internet and third parties

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests. It shares nothing with any third party, shows no ads, and offers no in-app purchases.

Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes all of it. There is nothing stored elsewhere to request deletion of.

Children

Invoice Maker is a business tool intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
